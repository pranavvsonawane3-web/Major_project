🧭 Healthcare Service Triage Guide

Unsure whether to visit an Emergency Room, an Urgent Care walk-in clinic, or schedule with a Primary Care doctor or specialist? Describe what you are experiencing below.

Common Scenarios:
🚨 Emergency Room (ER)
For life- or limb-threatening emergencies: severe chest pain, stroke symptoms, uncontrolled bleeding, severe breathing distress.
⚡ Urgent Care
For same-day walk-in needs: sprains, cuts needing stitches, earaches, urinary discomfort, minor burns, flu/strep tests.
🩺 Primary Care / Telehealth
For routine checkups, chronic health management (blood pressure, diabetes), prescription renewals, non-urgent symptoms.
🔬 Specialist
For organ-specific or complex conditions: Dermatology, Cardiology, Orthopedics, Neurology, Gastroenterology.

🎙️ What to tell the Emergency Dispatcher:

  1. State your location first: Address, cross streets, apartment or floor number.
  2. State the main issue: "The person is having crushing chest pain and trouble breathing."
  3. Stay on the line: Responders are dispatched while the operator talks to you. Do not hang up.
  4. Unlock front doors and turn on outside porch lights if safe to do so.

🚨 Emergency Quick Action
